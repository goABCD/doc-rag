# Macau Tourism and Economic Diversification (Demo Corpus)

This sample document is for demonstrating a local RAG pipeline. It is not official government text.

## Background
Macau's economy has long been concentrated in the gaming sector. Policy discussions often emphasize diversification into tourism services, conventions and exhibitions, finance, and technology-related services.

## Labor and Skills
When one industry dominates employment, labor supply and skill formation can become path-dependent. Students and workers may cluster into roles tied to gaming and hospitality, while data, analytics, and digital operations roles grow more slowly.

## Data Management Angle
Public statistical yearbooks, visitor arrivals, and industry share tables can be chunked, embedded, and retrieved to answer management questions such as: How did visitor structure change? Which sectors grew relative share? What risks follow from concentration?

## Using This File
Ask the RAG demo questions that can be answered from the paragraphs above. If the answer is not in the text, the model should say it does not know.
